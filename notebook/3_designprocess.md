# Design Process

---

## Overview

This section outlines the **tentative timeline and action plan** for the robot design and build process.  
The goal is to establish a clear, iterative path from **concept** to **competition-ready robot**, ensuring efficient teamwork, documentation, and testing.

---

## Timeline & Action Plan

| Phase | Key Activities | Deliverables |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **1. Brainstorm & Strategy Planning**  |- Review game manual and scoring strategies<br>- Identify key scoring objectives and possible designs<br>- Sketch initial robot concepts | - Strategy summary<br>- Sketches and design ideas |
| **2. Reference & Research** | - Analyze previous VEX designs and online references<br>- Study rule constraints and build limits<br>- Review CAD libraries and motor configurations | - Reference folder<br>- Viable design shortlist |
| **3. CAD Design & Virtual Prototyping** | - Create detailed CAD model in Fusion 360 or Onshape<br>- Validate mechanism fit, clearance, and motion<br>- Prepare materials and part list | - Final CAD assembly<br>- Bill of Materials (BOM) |
| **4. Mechanical Build Phase** | - Fabricate subassemblies (drive, intake, lift, etc.)<br>- Integrate motors, sensors, and control systems<br>- Conduct preliminary testing | - Assembled robot<br>- Functional mechanism tests |
| **5. Testing & Iteration** | - Perform driving and scoring tests<br>- Refine autonomous and driver control code<br>- Adjust mechanical components as needed | - Calibrated robot<br>- Documented test data |
| **6. Final Review & Documentation**| - Finalize robot design notebook entries<br>- Prepare for engineering interview<br>- Document design evolution and team roles | - Engineering notebook<br>- Presentation materials |

---

## Notes

- Each phase should conclude with **documentation updates** in the team’s Engineering Notebook.  
- Encourage **cross-phase collaboration** — brainstorming and CAD should involve both mechanical and programming subteams.  
- Regular **design reviews** every two weeks help maintain progress and catch issues early.

---



> **Reminder:** Document every iteration — even failed ideas — to show engineering reasoning and progress for judges.
